Nigerian Cuisine

Nigerian cuisine is diverse, spicy, and rich in flavors, featuring staple foods like yams, cassava, rice, and plantains, complemented by soups, stews, and grilled meats that reflect the country's ethnic and regional diversity.

Pizza

Pizza (Italian: ["pittsa"], Neapolitan: ["pittsa"]) is a savoury dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened, wheat-based dough, topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients (such as anchovies, mushrooms, onions, olives, pineapple, meat, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven. [1] A small pizza is sometimes called a pizzeta. A person who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.
